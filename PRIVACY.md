# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 19, 2026*

LiteMail is a mail app that runs entirely on your Mac. **It collects no data.** There is no
LiteMail server, no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your mail.** LiteMail downloads the messages you choose to keep (the last N days you set for
  each account) straight from your mail provider to your Mac, and stores them in the app's own
  folder. Older mail stays on your provider's servers.
- **Your passwords.** App-specific passwords are stored in the macOS keychain, readable only by
  LiteMail.
- **Settings**, such as how many days to keep and which addresses you send from.

Removing an account deletes its local mail and password. Deleting the app deletes everything.

## Who LiteMail talks to

Only the mail servers of the accounts you add (for example `imap.mail.me.com` and
`smtp.mail.me.com` for iCloud, or `imap.gmail.com` and `smtp.gmail.com` for Gmail), over
encrypted connections. Searching older mail asks those same servers; the messages you open are
kept in memory and never written to disk.

## Remote content

Remote images in email can tell the sender that you opened a message. LiteMail blocks them by
default and never runs JavaScript in email. Loading images for a message is your choice, one
message at a time (or always, if you turn that on in Settings).

## Children

LiteMail is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
